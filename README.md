# Mantella Living World - World

What's happening in Skyrim and what you've done, told to Mantella NPCs. A module of [Mantella Living World - Integration Hub](https://github.com/Abhisek571/Mantella-Living-World-Hub).

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| What NPCs know about you | Each NPC is told what they would know: your fame in their hold, your bounty (guards always; everyone in the hold once it is 1000 gold or more), Thane titles and quest outcomes whose word reached them (their hold, their guild or side, or people named in it). One short block, the same for the whole talk. Switch it off with "Tell NPCs what they would know about you" in the Hub MCM. With Mantella Living World - Knowledge installed, Dragonborn news is left to Knowledge |
| Quest outcomes | Main quest, Civil War, Companions, College, Thieves Guild, Dark Brotherhood, Thane titles and Riverwood and Whiterun side quests, each told only to NPCs who would know |
| Since we last met | An NPC you meet on a later day hears what changed since last time: new titles, war side, bounty, fame. At most 3 items, bounty and fame only when in the same hold as last time. Events from before you installed are not announced |
| What you did to them | An NPC who joins a talk hears what you did to them first hand, at most four things: caught you stealing from them or picking their pocket, you wearing something stolen from them, you just woke them up, you struck them, gifts (gold included; the last three, 30 days), trade with them today (what you paid and what they paid you, both counted), and a merchant whose chest is low on coin after trading with you (read from the chest as it is now, for 2 days). Deaths are recorded for Relationships' grief. Followers only count gold as a gift, once, as what is left with them when you close their inventory (giving gold and taking it back is nothing; the rest is them carrying your things); gold under 50 is remembered but not scored; gifts through the M.A.R.A.S gift menu count for followers too; training fees and dressing a mannequin are not gifts. Sneaking up on a sleeper (the pickpocket menu) does not count as waking them |
| What happened, for certain | Game events are kept as exact facts in the NPC's file, which the Integration Hub always shows them with the date: a merchant's lifetime trade with you (gold spent and sold, visits on different days, what you sell most), healing each other (once a minute at most, so healing over time counts once), saving each other's life (you heal a party member in bleedout or kill what downed them within 15 seconds; a party member kills what last hit you below a quarter of your health), dungeons cleared together (count and the latest), the strongest enemy faced together and the strongest you have beaten (dragons, then named people, then level), kills while together, friendly fire that cost health, fights fought side by side and fights where they struck what was attacking you. Party members are followers set as teammates who are close by and not told to wait. Fights you started with your own crime (guards and townsfolk you turned on) and people you attacked while they were peaceful are no enemies beaten. Kept for named NPCs only (generic guards and citizens share one file). Written when the NPC joins a talk, otherwise at most every 30 seconds |
| Hiding | Sneaking unseen, or a hood, helmet or mask in a hold where you are not famous or Thane: the NPC does not recognise you. Friends, family (spouse, adopted children, your steward), followers, teammates and anyone who has seen your face in an earlier talk always do, and uncanny observers see through it. Outside any hold a hood hides nothing. Someone who does not recognise you is not told what changed since you last met |
| Who knows whom | In group talks, one line per pair: strangers, know of, acquainted, close |
| Party banter | Party members (followers set as teammates, for example with NFF) react to a voiced line nearby, to you entering an inn and to the end of a fight you were hit in. Needs a party member and the Hub's pacing limits apply (banter every 2 minutes at most). A banter talk ends after 45 seconds |
| Initiative | An NPC with a reason walks up to you and starts a talk. This needs another module that gives NPCs a reason, such as Mantella Living World - Missives. Wait 10 minutes between approaches. Nobody walks up while you ride, sprint, are in beast form or held in a scene, and NPCs in a quest scene are left alone; the reason is kept until they have spoken. Switch it off with "Come over to talk" |
| SettleCrime | Guards settle a non-violent bounty: pay (asks you Yes/No first), bribe (asks you Yes/No first; 1.5 times the bounty, down to 1 times with Speech 100; you keep the stolen goods) or persuade (100 gold or less, and only if your Speech is at least the bounty). Thanes and well-known players (fame 5 or more) get a bounty of 40 gold or less waived. Waiver and persuade share one free clear a day per hold, and both take any stolen goods you carry, as paying does. Violent crime is never cleared. Bribe and persuade need Mantella's Advanced Actions on; without it a guard can only take payment |
| ReportWitnessedCrime | Guards add a bounty only when another NPC (not one of your followers) is present in the talk as a witness (so never in a one-on-one talk) and the game backs it: murder needs someone who was not hostile that you killed in that guard's hold in the last 3 days (refused when your violent bounty there is already 1000 or more), assault someone else in the talk (not the guard, not a follower) you struck within a day; trespassing and lockpicking need only the witness. Theft and pickpocketing cannot be reported: the game already adds that bounty when you are caught. Each crime counts once per talk, and reports add at most the vanilla bounty for that crime per hold each day (trespassing or lockpicking 5, assault 40, murder 1000). Needs Advanced Actions on (it picks the crime) |

Turn off Mantella's own AbsolveCrime action (off by default). Mantella's own ReportCrime is a separate action with a different name; keep it off as well (also off by default). Turning it off does not affect ReportWitnessedCrime.

Requires:
- Mantella Living World - Integration Hub
- [Mantella](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/98631) 0.14 with [Mantella - Direct AI Connect](https://github.com/Abhisek571/Mantella-Anthropic-API), which the Hub extension and guard-only actions need
- Advanced Actions on in Mantella (Actions tab of its settings) for bribe, persuade and ReportWitnessedCrime
- SKSE and [PapyrusUtil SE](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/13048)
- [powerofthree's Papyrus Extender](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/22854)
- [Papyrus MessageBox - SKSE NG](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/83578)
- [Address Library for SKSE Plugins](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/32444)

Optional:
- A follower framework that sets party members as teammates (NFF or vanilla followers) for banter
- Mantella Living World - Missives (or another module that gives NPCs a reason) for initiative
- Mantella Living World - Knowledge for Dragonborn news and quest state

For other modules: World sends the mod event `MLW_ScoreEvent` (Form npc, string kind, float weight; kind deeds, care or gifts) when the player heals or saves a party member, clears a dungeon or fights beside them, and for gifts. Nothing needs to listen.

Build: the Hub's `tools/build.py --only world` (the SKSE plugin first, with `plugin/build.ps1`). Data: `py -3 tools/build_quest_outcomes.py` (reads Quest Journal Overhaul's quest files for holds, only needed to rebuild the data), tests in `tools/test_build_quest_outcomes.py` and `test/check_events.py`; `py -3 tools/make_records.py` writes the plugin YAML and action files.
